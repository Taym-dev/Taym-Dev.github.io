Privacy Policy - Tamerr Clips

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Tamerr Clips is a personal tool that uploads short video clips to the TikTok account of its owner. It is used only by its owner.

1. What data the app uses

  • The TikTok access token and refresh token of the owner's account, to upload videos.
  • Basic TikTok account information (user.info.basic), to identify the account.
  • The video files and descriptions that the owner uploads.

    • 2. How the data is stored

    All data is stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

    3. Data of other people

    The app does not collect data of other TikTok users or viewers.

    4. Deleting data

    The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services). Stored tokens are then deleted from the owner's computer.
